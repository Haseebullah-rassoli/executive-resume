# Executive Resume Builder

A free, browser-based executive resume builder for organizing leadership experience, professional achievements, skills, and career history into a clear, ATS-friendly resume.

**Live website:** [Open the Executive Resume Builder](https://haseebullah-rassoli.github.io/executive-resume/)

## About

Executive Resume Builder is a practical career tool for experienced professionals who need to present leadership scope, strategic impact, career history, and professional achievements clearly.

The executive CV builder uses guided prompts and a live preview to help users create a focused leadership resume with an ATS-friendly, single-column structure.

## Features

- Live executive resume preview
- ATS-friendly leadership resume structure
- Guided sections for executive summary, core competencies, professional experience, board or advisory work, education, and certifications
- Prompts that emphasize strategic outcomes and measurable achievements
- PDF and Word document export
- Browser-based use with no account required
- Mobile-friendly interface
- Resume information remains in your browser and is not uploaded by the application

## Who It Is For

- Senior professionals
- Managers and organizational leaders
- Executives preparing an executive CV
- Applicants who need to communicate leadership scope and professional achievements
